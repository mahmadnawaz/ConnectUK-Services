Our Complaints Procedure

Clear, Fair, and Fast Resolution

At ConnectUk Services, we are committed to delivering the highest level of customer service throughout your contract.

If you're unhappy with any part of our service, we want to put it right as soon as possible.

Step 1 – Submitting Complaint

Email: info@connectukservices.com

Include a detailed description and preferred resolution.

Step 2 – Acknowledgement

You will receive acknowledgment within five business days.

Step 3 – Our Response

Final response or a holding response explaining any delay.

Step 4 – Further Review

If not satisfied, we are happy to reconsider with new information.

Step 5 – Final Deadline

A final response will be issued within 8 weeks total.

Step 6 – Independent Resolution

Escalate to the Dispute Resolution Ombudsman if unresolved.
